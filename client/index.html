Welcome to your district's data portal

This dashboard is built on data already published by the Georgia Office of Student Achievement (GOSA) — assessment results, attendance, graduation rates, and financial efficiency metrics that every school and district in Georgia reports publicly each year. Nothing here is private or new; this tool exists to make that public data easier to explore, compare, and share.

It's built for schools, districts, and universities to stand up their own branded version of this dashboard — same underlying data, styled with your colors and name — so you can put clear, credible performance data in front of your community without building a reporting tool from scratch.
